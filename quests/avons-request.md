# Avon's Request

**Quest Giver:** Avon, senior cleric of the Church of Lathander in Westbridge
**Location:** Church of Lathander (given privately during the Eclipse Festival)
**Status:** Accepted

## Summary

Avon has asked the party to travel into the Kryptgarden Forest and investigate the Temple of Selûne. The temple's clergy were forced to abandon the site after a creeping, necrotic corruption took hold. Avon believes a powerful seal within the temple has been broken, unleashing the undead that now plague the forest. Priestess Elara, Avon's childhood friend and the temple's caretaker, has gone missing — and hunters who venture toward the temple have not returned.

Avon insisted the party wait until the Eclipse Ritual is complete before departing, prioritizing the town's protection over an immediate rescue. This decision drew criticism from Tethys, who questioned whether the ritual should take precedence over Elara's safety.

## Objectives

- Travel to the Temple of Selûne in the Kryptgarden Forest
- Investigate the source of the necrotic corruption
- Determine the fate of Priestess Elara
- Restore the broken seal, if possible

## Key Details

- The party hired **Snagg** as a forest guide for 100gp
- **Kaeleen** confirmed 6-7 hunting parties have failed to reach the temple
- Hunters who did return reported **bloodless corpses** — wounds from blades, but no blood (a sign of the Heralds of Hadar's Crimson Blades)
- Avon was abducted by the Heralds during the Eclipse Ritual before the party could depart, complicating the quest's priorities

## Rewards

**The Dawnray Spear**
A +1 spear that shines with the light of the morning sun when it strikes. On a hit, it illuminates a 30ft radius with dawn-light and deals an additional 1d6 radiant damage to all creatures within 5ft of the target. Against undead, it deals an extra 1d8 radiant damage.

**Lathander's Boon**
Avon performs a ritual to bestow a divine boon upon one party member. That character can cast *Aid* once (as a 3rd-level slot) without components. This use recharges each morning, but only if the character can see the light of dawn.

**A Charter of the Dawn**
An official charter recognizing the party as sworn friends of the Church of Lathander. It grants:
- Free modest lodging at any Church of Lathander temple
- Free healing services at any Church of Lathander temple
- The ability to request an audience with church leaders throughout the realm
- Possible concessions from allied organizations such as the Lord's Alliance
