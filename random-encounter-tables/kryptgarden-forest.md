# Kryptgarden Forest Random Encounters

This table can be used for random encounters within the Kryptgarden Forest.

---

### d12 Encounters

---

### 1. The Dragon's Shadow

- Old Gnawbone flies overhead, observing her territory — not hostile
- A plate-sized green scale falls at the party's feet
- **Dragon's Scale:** Can be embedded into a shield/armor (8 hrs, Smith's/Leatherworker's/Woodcarver's Tools) to grant resistance to poison damage. Consumes the scale.

### 2. The Hunter's Predicament

- Abandoned campsite; signs of a struggle
- DC 12 Survival: two sets of tracks (human + large clawed biped) lead to a cave
- A **Troll** is inside, debating how to cook a hunter stuffed in a burlap sack
- DC 13 Stealth to surprise the troll
- **Corbin** (human Scout, 1 HP) — grateful, offers to repay the debt later
- **Loot:** 45 gp, silvered shortsword, Corbin's map to a hidden supply cache

### 3. The Forgotten Shrine

- Crescent-shaped clearing with a crumbling Selûnite shrine
- Silver box with rotating moon phase symbols
- Inscription: *"From darkness I am born anew, to glorious light I ascend. My five faces watch the night, speak my silvered cycle."*
- **Solution:** Speak in order: "New Moon, Waxing Crescent, First Quarter, Waxing Gibbous, Full Moon"
- Wrong answer: symbols fade, must wait until next dusk to retry
- **Reward (DM's choice):**
  - **Crescent Charm:** Cast *Moonbeam* once per night (DC 13)
  - **Starlight Cloak:** Advantage on Stealth in dim light/darkness; cast *Faerie Fire* once per dusk (DC 13)
  - **Shield of the Lunar Watch:** Bonus action to shed dim light 10 ft; reaction to grant advantage on 1 saving throw to self or creature within 10 ft

### 4. The Fungi Grove

- Massive glowing mushrooms; fungus-infected humanoids (hunters/loggers) shamble toward the party
- **Hazard:** Creatures starting their turn in the grove: DC 11 Con save or poisoned for 1 min
- **Loot:** 34 gp, finely made handaxe, *Potion of Growth*
- DC 14 Nature: harvest mushrooms usable as *Potion of Growth* components

### 5. The Verdant Vendor (Merchant)

- **Fell** — firbolg merchant seated on a mossy stone, slow and deliberate, calm grey eyes
- **Wares:**
  - **Seed of Awakening:** Plant in fertile ground, concentrate 1 min — sprouts into a friendly **Awakened Shrub** that obeys commands
  - **Whisperwood Flute:** Cast *Speak with Plants* (2 charges; at dawn, roll 1d2 — on a 2, regains 2 charges)
  - **Grasping Arrows (5):** On hit, speak command word — target must DC 14 Str save or be restrained 1 min (action to repeat save)

### 6. The Huntsman and the Hind

- **Gideon Hawthorn** (Knight on Warhorse) — vain, golden-haired hunter chasing a celestial stag
- **Help Gideon:** 25 gp reward; disadvantage on Cha checks with fey/druids for 1 tenday
- **Help the stag:** DC 15 Deception/Intimidation to convince Gideon to leave; he flees combat below half HP
- **Reward for saving stag:** **Moon-touched Weapon** appears at next campsite

### 7. The Weaver's Ambush

- Web-choked grove; cocooned humanoid shapes; a silver circlet gleams in a shaft of light
- **Webs:** Difficult terrain; DC 12 Dex save or restrained (DC 12 Str action to escape)
- DC 15 Perception to spot spiders in canopy (prevents surprise)
- **Enemies:** 2 **Giant Spiders** + 4 **Spider Swarms**
- **Loot:** 2 **Giant Spider Venom**, **Circlet of Verdant Shaping** (action to shape 20-ft cube of plant life into simple forms for 1 hr; uses = proficiency bonus, recharges at dawn)

### 8. The Grumpy Herbalist

- **Fendrel** — crotchety human alchemist in a ramshackle hut, exiled from Longsaddle
- Rude but not evil; needs **giant spider venom** (connects to encounter 7)
- **Reward for venom:** Player's choice of **Potion of Superior Healing**, **Potion of Invisibility**, or **Potion of Speed**
- No venom? He'll task them with getting some

### 9. The Corpse Violets

- Clearing carpeted with deep purple violets; elven skeleton clutching a silver locket
- 2 **Ghouls** chanting over the skeleton, tracing glowing purple runes
- If not stopped in 3 rounds: ghouls become **Empowered** (+10 HP, +1d6 necrotic on attacks)
- Damage to a ghoul: DC 10 Con save to maintain ritual concentration
- When party reveals themselves: 4 **Zombies** erupt from the soil
- DC 14 Religion/History: violets are parasitic fungus thriving on necrotic energy; ghouls were once elves cursed for cannibalism
- **Loot:** 80 gp, **Memento Mori** (raven skull; cast *Speak with Dead*, 1 charge; at dusk roll 1d2, on a 2 regains 1 charge)

### 10. The Whispering Tree

- Massive bone-white petrified tree; faces swirl beneath the bark; elven script encircles an anguished face
- Inscription (Elvish): *"Three voices bound in sorrow's grasp / Two woven of deceit / One truth alone will break the clasp / And grant a soul's retreat."*
- **Puzzle — The Liar's Lament:** Three sister spirits; one tells the truth, two must lie. Identify the truthful one.
  - **Lia:** "I was the Sentinel... a truth that Faerel's honest heart surely knows."
  - **Faerel:** "Lia grieves, but her memory is clouded. Our sister Nael was the true Sentinel."
  - **Nael:** "I was never the Sentinel... only the true Sentinel can speak the truth."
- **Solution:** Touch the tree and say "Faerel speaks the truth"
- **Wrong answer:** 3 **Specters** (ghostly elven warriors) attack; puzzle resets after they're destroyed
- **Reward: Amulet of the Sister Sentinels** (uncommon) — Once per long rest, bonus action to invoke one spirit for 1 min:
  - **Lia:** Place spectral thorns in a 5-ft square within 30 ft; DC 15 Str or restrained; bonus action to move the patch each turn
  - **Faerel:** See a silvery outline of one creature within 30 ft (even through walls, 60 ft range); advantage on attacks against it
  - **Nael:** Create immobile spectral duplicate within 30 ft; bonus action to swap places with it

### 11. The Wounded Satyr

- **Filo** (satyr) — leg caught in a goblin trap, 3 **Goblins** taunting him
- Goblins flee if one is killed; easily surprised
- **Reward:** Enchanted wine — drinker gains *Bless* for 1 hour
- Filo warns: "Stay clear of the old temple to the north. Something dark stirs in there."

### 12. The Ancient Battlefield

- Boggy clearing littered with rusted weapons, shattered shields, skeletal remains
- **Curse:** Disturbing a grave or taking an item: DC 14 Wis save or cursed (disadvantage on attacks and saves until *Remove Curse* or item is returned with an apology)
- DC 16 Investigation: find an untarnished **+1 Longsword** in a fallen knight's hand
- **Curse bypass:** Perform a rite of passage for the knight (prayer, burial) before taking the sword
