**Proficiency Bonus** +2

## Traits
***Nimble Escape.*** Snagg can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of his turns.

***Favored Enemy (Undead).*** Snagg has advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks to track undead, as well as on Intelligence checks to recall information about them. (DM note: swap to *beasts* if preferred — fits his hunter archetype; *undead* picked for current Kryptgarden threat.)

***Natural Explorer (Forest).*** While traveling for an hour or more in forest terrain, Snagg gains the following benefits: difficult terrain doesn't slow his group's travel, his group can't become lost except by magical means, he remains alert to danger even while tracking/navigating/foraging, he can move stealthily at a normal pace when alone, he finds twice as much food while foraging, and while tracking he learns the exact number, sizes, and how long ago creatures passed through. He also doubles his proficiency bonus on Intelligence and Wisdom checks related to forest creatures and terrain.

***Archery Fighting Style.*** Snagg gains a +2 bonus to attack rolls he makes with ranged weapons (already included).

***Ranger's Companion.*** Snagg's animal companion, [Noodle](/creatures/noodle.md), acts on his initiative and obeys his commands. Noodle adds Snagg's proficiency bonus (+2) to its AC, attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and any skills it is proficient in; its hit point maximum equals the higher of its own or four times Snagg's ranger level (12). Snagg can use his action to command Noodle to take the Attack, Dash, Disengage, or Help action; if Snagg doesn't issue a command, Noodle takes the Dodge action.

***Spellcasting.*** Snagg is a 3rd-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following ranger spells prepared:

1st level (3 slots): *cure wounds*, *ensnaring strike*, *goodberry*, *hunter's mark*

## Actions
***Longbow.*** Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage.

***Shortsword.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

## Bonus Actions
***Hunter's Mark (Concentration, up to 1 hour).*** Snagg chooses a creature he can see within 90 feet and marks it as his quarry. Until the spell ends, he deals an extra 1d6 damage to the target when he hits it with a weapon attack, and he has advantage on any Wisdom (Perception) or Wisdom (Survival) check he makes to find it. If the target drops to 0 hit points before the spell ends, Snagg can use a bonus action on a subsequent turn to mark a new creature.

***Nimble Escape.*** Snagg takes the Disengage or Hide action.
