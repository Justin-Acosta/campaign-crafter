# Willow (Herald of Hadar)

[Ritual Abduction Combat Encounter](../combat-encounters/session-1/ritual-abduction.md)

*Medium humanoid (tiefling), chaotic neutral*

**Armor Class** 18 (studded leather, 18 with *mage armor*)
**Hit Points** 120 (12d8 + 24)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 20 (+5) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +9
**Skills** Arcana +7, Deception +9, Persuasion +9
**Damage Resistances** Fire
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 11
**Languages** Common, Infernal, Deep Speech
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

## Traits

**Agonizing Blast.** When Willow casts *eldritch blast*, add her Charisma modifier to the damage it deals on a hit.

**Devil's Sight.** Magical darkness doesn't impede Willow's darkvision.

**Fey Ancestry.** Willow has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

**Spellcasting.** Willow is a 12th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks). She regains her expended spell slots when he finishes a short or long rest. She knows the following Warlock spells:
*   Cantrips (at will): *eldritch blast*, *mage hand*, *minor illusion*, *prestidigitation*
*   1st–5th level (3 5th-level slots): *banishment*, *counterspell*, *darkness*, *dimension door*, *fly*, *hunger of hadar*, *hypnotic pattern*, *misty step*, *scrying*, *synaptic static*

**Special Spellcasting.** Willow can cast the following spells without expending a spell slot:
*   1/day: *plane shift* (self and up to 8 willing creatures only)

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Willow makes two Dagger attacks.

**Dagger.** *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) psychic damage.

**Eldritch Blast.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 300 ft. (Eldritch Spear), three beams. *Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) force damage per beam.

**Dark Delirium (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** Willow chooses a creature that she can see within 60 feet of her. The creature must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be charmed or frightened by Willow (her choice) for 1 minute or until Willow's concentration is broken (as if concentrating on a spell). This effect ends early if the creature takes any damage. Until this illusion ends, the creature thinks it is lost in a misty realm, the appearance of which Willow chooses. The creature can see and hear only itself, Willow, and the illusion.
