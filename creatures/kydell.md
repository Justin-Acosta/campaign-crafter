# Kydell (Herald of Hadar)

[Ritual Abduction Combat Encounter](../sessions/session-2/ritual-abduction.md)

*Medium humanoid (goliath), lawful evil*

**Armor Class** 18 (Plate Armor)
**Hit Points** 150 (12d8 + 48)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +8
**Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +8, Perception +5
**Damage Resistances** Cold; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks (from *Stones Endurance* reaction)
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
**Languages** Common, Giant, Deep Speech
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

## Traits

**Stone's Endurance (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** When Kydell takes damage, he can use his reaction to roll a d12. Add his Constitution modifier to the number rolled, and reduce the damage by that total.

**Devil's Sight.** Magical darkness doesn't impede Kydell's darkvision.

**Innate Spellcasting.** Kydell's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma. He can cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
*   At will: *detect magic*, *levitate*

**Spellcasting.** Kydell is a 12th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). He regains his expended spell slots when he finishes a short or long rest. He knows the following Warlock spells:
*   Cantrips (at will): *eldritch blast*, *toll the dead*, *mage hand*
*   1st–5th level (3 5th-level slots): *armor of agathys*, *counterspell*, *darkness*, *hunger of hadar*, *misty step*, *shield*, *sickening radiance*, *staggering smite*, *synaptic static*

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Kydell makes two attacks with his Greatsword.

**Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage.

**Eldritch Blast.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +8 to hit, range 120 ft., three beams. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) force damage per beam.

**Eldritch Smite (1/Turn).** When Kydell hits a creature with his greatsword, he can expend a warlock spell slot to deal an extra 27 (6d8) force damage to the target and knock it prone if it is Huge or smaller.

## Reactions

**Parry.** Kydell adds 3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Kydell must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

## Spell Quick Reference

**Sickening Radiance** — *4th-level evocation*
*Casting Time:* 1 action | *Range:* 120 ft. | *Components:* V, S | *Duration:* Concentration, up to 10 minutes

Dim, greenish light spreads within a 30-foot-radius sphere centered on a point you choose within range. The light spreads around corners. When a creature moves into the spell's area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it must succeed on a **Constitution saving throw** or take **4d10 radiant damage**, suffer **one level of exhaustion**, and emit a dim greenish light in a 5-foot radius. This light makes it impossible for the creature to benefit from being invisible. The light and any levels of exhaustion caused by this spell go away when the spell ends.

**Staggering Smite** — *4th-level evocation*
*Casting Time:* 1 bonus action | *Range:* Self | *Components:* V | *Duration:* Concentration, up to 1 minute

The next time you hit a creature with a melee weapon attack during this spell's duration, your weapon pierces both body and mind. The attack deals an extra **4d6 psychic damage** to the target. The target must make a **Wisdom saving throw**. On a failed save, it has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks, and can't take reactions, until the end of its next turn.

**Synaptic Static** — *5th-level enchantment*
*Casting Time:* 1 action | *Range:* 120 ft. | *Components:* V, S | *Duration:* Instantaneous

You choose a point within range and cause psychic energy to explode there. Each creature in a 20-foot-radius sphere must make an **Intelligence saving throw**. A creature with an Intelligence score of 2 or lower can't be affected. On a failed save, a target takes **8d6 psychic damage** (half on success). After a failed save, a target has muddled thoughts for 1 minute — it rolls a **d6 and subtracts** the number rolled from all its attack rolls, ability checks, and Constitution saving throws to maintain concentration. The target can repeat the Intelligence save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
