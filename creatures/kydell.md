# Kydell (Herald of Hadar)

[Ritual Abduction Combat Encounter](../combat-encounters/session-1/ritual-abduction.md)

*Medium humanoid (goliath), lawful evil*

**Armor Class** 18 (Plate Armor)
**Hit Points** 150 (12d8 + 48)
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Wis +5, Cha +8
**Skills** Athletics +8, Intimidation +8, Perception +5
**Damage Resistances** Cold; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks (from *Stones Endurance* reaction)
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 15
**Languages** Common, Giant, Deep Speech
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

## Traits

**Stone's Endurance (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** When Kydell takes damage, he can use his reaction to roll a d12. Add his Constitution modifier to the number rolled, and reduce the damage by that total.

**Devil's Sight.** Magical darkness doesn't impede Kydell's darkvision.

**Innate Spellcasting.** Kydell's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma. He can cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
*   At will: *detect magic*, *levitate*

**Spellcasting.** Kydell is a 12th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). He regains his expended spell slots when he finishes a short or long rest. He knows the following Warlock spells:
*   Cantrips (at will): *eldritch blast*, *toll the dead*, *mage hand*
*   1st–5th level (3 5th-level slots): *armor of agathys*, *counterspell*, *darkness*, *dimension door*, *hunger of hadar*, *misty step*, *shield*, *sickening radiance*, *staggering smite*, *synaptic static*

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Kydell makes two attacks with his Greatsword.

**Greatsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) slashing damage plus 4 (1d8) necrotic damage.

**Eldritch Blast.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +8 to hit, range 120 ft., three beams. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) force damage per beam.

**Eldritch Smite (1/Turn).** When Kydell hits a creature with his greatsword, he can expend a warlock spell slot to deal an extra 27 (6d8) force damage to the target and knock it prone if it is Huge or smaller.

## Reactions

**Parry.** Kydell adds 3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Kydell must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.
