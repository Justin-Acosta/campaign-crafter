## Traits
***Keen Smell.*** The bear has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

## Actions
***Multiattack.*** The bear makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its claws.

***Bite.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: (1d6 + 4) piercing damage. DC 12 WIS saving throw or cursed:
  **Necrotic Rot:** Disadvantage on all perception checks.
