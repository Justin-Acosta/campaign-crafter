# Skeleton Acolyte
*Medium Undead, lawful evil*
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)

**Armor Class** 12 (tattered robes)
**Hit Points** 27 (5d8 + 5)
**Speed** 30 ft.

|    STR    |    DEX    |    CON    |    INT    |    WIS    |    CHA    |
|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|
|  10 (+0)  |  14 (+2)  |  12 (+1)  |  14 (+2)  |  10 (+0)  |   6 (-2)  |

**Skills** Arcana +4
**Damage Vulnerabilities** bludgeoning
**Damage Immunities** poison
**Condition Immunities** exhaustion, poisoned
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak

## Traits
***Spellcasting.*** The skeleton is a 3rd-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following wizard spells prepared:
*   Cantrips (at will): *chill touch, mage hand*
*   1st level (4 slots): *false life, ray of sickness, shield*
*   2nd level (2 slots): *ray of enfeeblement, wither and bloom*

## Actions
***Dagger.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

***Chill Touch.*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +4 to hit, range 120 ft., one creature. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) necrotic damage, and the target can't regain hit points until the start of the skeleton's next turn. If the target is undead, it also has disadvantage on attack rolls against the skeleton until the end of the skeleton's next turn.

## Spell Descriptions

***False Life (1st Level).*** Casting Time: 1 action. Range: Self. Duration: 1 hour. The skeleton gains 1d4 + 4 temporary hit points.

***Ray of Sickness (1st Level).*** Casting Time: 1 action. Range: 60 ft. Make a ranged spell attack (+4 to hit). On a hit, the target takes 2d8 poison damage and must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw. On a failed save, the target is also poisoned until the end of the skeleton's next turn.

***Shield (1st Level).*** Casting Time: 1 reaction (when hit by an attack or magic missile). Range: Self. Duration: 1 round. The skeleton gains a +5 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and takes no damage from magic missile.

***Ray of Enfeeblement (2nd Level).*** Casting Time: 1 action. Range: 60 ft. Make a ranged spell attack (+4 to hit). On a hit, the target deals only half damage with weapon attacks that use Strength until the spell ends. At the end of each of the target's turns, it can make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw against the spell. On a success, the spell ends.

***Wither and Bloom (2nd Level).*** Casting Time: 1 action. Range: 60 ft. Radius: 10 ft. Duration: Instantaneous.
Creatures of your choice in the area must make a DC 12 Constitution saving throw, taking 2d6 necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success. One creature of your choice in that area can spend and roll one of its unspent Hit Dice and regain hit points equal to the roll plus your spellcasting ability modifier (+2).
