# Noodle
![Noodle](/creatures/images/noodle.png)

**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)

*Large Beast, unaligned*
**Armor Class** 15 (natural armor + proficiency)
**Hit Points** 16 (3d10)
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 40 ft.

|    STR    |    DEX    |    CON    |    INT    |    WIS    |    CHA    |
|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|:---------:|
|  14 (+2)  |  16 (+3)  |  12 (+1)  |   1 (-5)  |  10 (+0)  |   3 (-4)  |

**Saving Throws** DEX +5, CON +3
**Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +5
**Senses** blindsight 30 ft., darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** —

## Traits
***Companion Bond.*** Add the ranger's proficiency bonus (+2) to Noodle's AC, attack rolls, damage rolls, saving throws, and skills (already included).

***Segmented Body.*** Noodle has advantage on saving throws against being grappled or restrained.

## Actions
***Bite.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one creature. Hit: 7 (1d8 + 3) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 11 Constitution saving throw or be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

***Constrict.*** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one Medium or smaller creature. Hit: 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage, and the target is grappled (escape DC 12). Until the grapple ends, the target is restrained, and Noodle can't constrict another target.
