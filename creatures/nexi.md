# Nexi (Herald of Hadar)

[Ritual Abduction Combat Encounter](../combat-encounters/session-1/ritual-abduction.md)

*Medium humanoid (half-elf), neutral evil*

**Armor Class** 17 (studded leather, high dex)
**Hit Points** 120 (12d8 + 24)
**Speed** 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 12 (+1) | 20 (+5) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +5
**Skills** Acrobatics +9, Perception +6, Stealth +13
**Damage Resistances** Necrotic
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Deep Speech
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)

## Traits

**Assassinate.** During their first turn, Nexi has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. Any hit Nexi scores against a surprised creature is a critical hit.

**Cunning Action.** On each of their turns, Nexi can use a bonus action to take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

**Evasion.** If Nexi is subjected to an effect that allows them to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, Nexi instead takes no damage if they succeed on the saving throw, and only half damage if they fail.

**Sneak Attack (1/Turn).** Nexi deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage when they hit a target with a weapon attack and have advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of Nexi that isn't incapacitated and Nexi doesn't have disadvantage on the attack roll.

**Spellcasting.** Nexi is a 12th-level spellcaster. Their spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16, +8 to hit with spell attacks). They regain their expended spell slots when they finish a short or long rest. They know the following Warlock spells:
*   Cantrips (at will): *eldritch blast*, *message*, *minor illusion*
*   1st–5th level (3 5th-level slots): *armor of agathys*, *darkness*, *dimension door*, *hunger of hadar*, *invisibility*, *misty step*, *shadow of moil*, *spider climb*

## Actions

**Multiattack.** Nexi makes two attacks with their Shortsword.

**Shortsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage.

**Eldritch Blast.** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +8 to hit, range 120 ft., three beams. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) force damage per beam.

## Reactions

**Uncanny Dodge.** When an attacker that Nexi can see hits them with an attack, Nexi can use their reaction to halve the attack's damage against them.
