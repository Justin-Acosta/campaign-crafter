# Sumber Hills Lore

The Sumber Hills are a rust-red spine of windswept gullies and cave-studded escarpments where the Redclaw goblin clan rules from warrens threaded through the collapsed arches and shafts of ancient Tyar-Besil; marked by crimson handprints and bone totems, these cunning survivors use the maze-like ravines to vanish after lightning-fast ambushes, their survival owed to a keen understanding of the valley's unnatural storms and the secrets buried within their dwarven halls.

Red Larch and Beliard, the two towns closest to the Sumber Hills, have refused to trade with the goblins, and  Red Larch has even sent hunters out to kill goblins in the Sumber Hills.
