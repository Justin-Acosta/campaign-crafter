# Kryptgarden Forest Characters

### Elara
A Selunite wood elf priestess and childhood friend of Avon, Elara is currently missing after an undead infestation overran the Temple of Selune. Her disappearance is what drives Avon to seek the party's help.

### Claugiyliamatar
Claugiyliamatar, an ancient green dragon also known as "Old Gnawbone," resides in Kryptgarden Forest. Her massive form is covered in emerald scales, and a distinctive crest runs from just above her eyes down her spine. A perpetual, smug grin seems fixed on her face, often stained with the gore of her latest meal. Her voice is a low, rumbling hiss that sounds like grinding stones, laced with a cruel, ancient amusement.

She is a cunning and paranoid creature who delights in the suffering of others, a trait that earned her the name "Old Gnawbone" from her habit of gnawing on her victims long after they are dead. Claugiyliamatar rarely engages in direct combat, preferring to manipulate events from the shadows. She employs a network of agents to sow discord in nearby cities, and spends much of her time scrying on powerful individuals, nursing her envy and plotting their downfall. Her greatest desire is to find a way to walk among mortals in a human form, without sacrificing any of her draconic power.

### Fell
A firbolg of indeterminate age whose skin has the rough, textured appearance of weathered bark. Her long hair is a cascade of living green leaves and twigs, interwoven with small, white blossoms. Her movements are slow and deliberate, and her calm, grey eyes are placid and unreadable. Her voice is low and melodic, like the rustling of leaves. She is a merchant who sells items seemingly grown from the forest itself.

### Gideon Hawthorn
A human male of almost blinding handsomeness, with perfectly coiffed golden hair and a brilliant purple cape. His plate armor is so polished it reflects the forest canopy. He rides a proud, white charger and speaks in a rich, booming voice, carrying himself with an air of arrogant grace.

### Fendrel
A wiry, wild-haired human man of about 60. His beard is tangled, spectacles are perched on his nose, and his robes are covered in a hundred alchemical spills. He is a crotchety, impatient, and rude alchemist who lives as a hermit in the forest.

### Filo
A satyr with shaggy goat-like legs and small horns poking from his tangled brown hair. He is quick to anger and prone to furious, profane outbursts when threatened or trapped.
