# Cult of Hadar Characters

## Main Characters

### Kydell
Kydell is a hulking goliath, his powerful frame a shield for the fierce, protective loyalty he holds for Nora. He is clad in heavy, dark plate armor, the metal etched with grotesque designs that seem to writhe, but beneath the monstrous exterior is a simple, desperate goal: to protect the only person who ever mattered to him. He has always been a man of few words, a trait that became a source of anguish when a childhood tragedy stole Nora's voice. His thoughts, now broadcast through their shared psychic bond, are not just rage and hunger, but a constant, roaring fire of devotion and a deep-seated fear of losing her again. He sees the abduction of Avon not as an act of evil, but as a necessary evil—a sacrifice required to maintain the power he needs to keep Nora safe. He carries a greatsword.

### Willow
Willow is a lithe tiefling with skin the color of a deep twilight violet. She was a gifted, but overlooked, mage's apprentice, constantly dismissed because of her heritage. Burning with ambition and a desperate need to prove her worth, she sought out Hadar, who offered her the power she craved. She carries herself with a serpentine grace, a confident mask for her deep-seated insecurity. While her methods are ruthless, she often feels a pang of sympathy for her victims, seeing in them the same powerlessness she fought so hard to escape. She carries a dagger and staff.

### Nexi
Nexi is a half-elf, a being from the Shadowfell who has felt the soul-crushing despair of that plane. They sought out Hadar not for personal gain, but for the power to feel something—anything—other than the oppressive gloom of their home. The pact, and the hunger it brings, is a constant, sharp sensation that reminds them they are alive. They cling to the shadows, their movements twitchy and alert, not out of malice, but from a lifetime of learned caution. They view the abduction as a grim necessity, a task that must be done to keep the gnawing emptiness at bay.

### Nora
Nora is a gaunt drow woman. Her pale face is dominated by the scar tissue where her lower jaw once was, a brutal injury from her youth that left her permanently mute. For years, she could only communicate through frantic gestures. It was this desperate silence that drove Kydell to seek out Hadar's power, a choice she supported. The pact was a twisted answer to their prayers: it took her hands, her only way to speak to the world, but gave her a voice inside Kydell's mind. She endures the hunger and the whispers of Hadar as a price worth paying for this connection. While she understands the fear she inspires, she sees their actions as the only way to preserve the bond that saved them both from isolation.
