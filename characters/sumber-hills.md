# Sumber Hills Characters

## Main Characters

### Chief Grakka
Grakka is the matriarch of the Redclaw goblin clan, a wizened goblin with skin the color of dried clay and a milky white blind eye that she claims sees "the storms before they break." She wears a mismatched suit of armor cobbled together from ancient dwarven scraps found in the ruins of Tyar-Besil, clinking softly with every step. Her voice is a raspy croak that carries surprising authority, silencing the bickering of her clan with a single word. Grakka is a pragmatic survivor who values the lives of her people above all else. She maintains a tenuous peace with Captain Darian Hale of Westbridge, respecting his strength and fairness, but she holds a deep, simmering hatred for the people of Red Larch who hunt her kind for sport. She is not evil, but she is ruthless when it comes to defending the warrens.

## Side Characters

### Storm-Caller Zrix
A wiry goblin with hair that perpetually stands on end, as if charged with static. Zrix wears a necklace of fulgurites—glass formed by lightning strikes—and his fingers twitch with restless energy. He serves as the clan's shaman, interpreting the unnatural storms of the Sumber Hills. He speaks in rapid, thunderous whispers and is often seen shouting at the sky during gales.

### Vorn "The Butcher" Krell
A hulking human bounty hunter from Red Larch, Vorn is a man whose cruelty is etched into his scar-crossed face. He wears a grotesque necklace of dried goblin ears and carries a heavy crossbow that he treats with more tenderness than any living person. He views the Redclaw goblins as vermin to be exterminated and considers Captain Darian Hale a traitor to his race for harboring them.

### Thrum
A massive ogre with rust-colored skin that blends perfectly with the hills. Thrum serves as the heavy muscle for the Redclaw clan, though he is surprisingly gentle with the smaller goblins, often letting them ride on his shoulders. He wears a helmet fashioned from a discarded iron cauldron and wields a tree trunk reinforced with dwarven metal bands. despite his size, he is terrified of thunder and will hide in the deepest caves during storms.
