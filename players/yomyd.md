# Yomyd (Yom)

## 1. Stats
- **Race:** Tortle
- **Class:** Monk (Way of Mercy)
- **Deity:** Chauntea (Goddess of Agriculture)
- **Origin:** Chult (Ancestral), Sunset Mountains (Upbringing)

## 2. Motivations
- **Companionship:** Yom was kicked out of the [Abbey of the Golden Sheaf](../players/jareth-brightbolt.md#origin) alongside his friend [Jareth Brightbolt](../players/jareth-brightbolt.md). He seems to be sticking with Jareth as they navigate the world outside the monastery.
- **Managing the Curse:** He lives with a dark ancestral burden—the Curse of Yeenoghu—and likely seeks to control these abyssal impulses or find a way to break the pact his ancestor made.
- **Monastic Duty:** Having been raised in an Abbey dedicated to agriculture and protection, he likely retains a drive to protect others or guide them, even if he is no longer at the temple.

## 3. Flaws
- **Curse of Yeenoghu:** An ancestor made a pact with an abyssal entity, cursing their bloodline. If a reptilian animal (not humanoid/dragon) dies within his sight, he must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw**. On a failure, he hears abyssal whispers and is compelled to consume the creature.
- **Fearful Retreat:** His instincts override his discipline when he is frightened. When he gains the **Frightened condition**, his *Shell Defense* feature automatically activates, forcing him into his shell. He must make a **DC 10 Wisdom saving throw** at the start of his turn to emerge (though the fear persists).

## 4. Other Details
- **Abbey Life:** He was a monk at the **Abbey of the Golden Sheaf** in the Sunset Mountains, the same home as Jareth. He was expelled at the same time as Jareth (possibly as collateral damage or involvement in Jareth's explosive experiments).
- **Tortle Lore:** While Tortles are native to the tropical island of Chult and often worship the Sun and Moon, Yom found himself far from home in the mountains. His presence there (born from an egg? brought by parents?) sets him apart from his kin.
- **Timeline:** Has been in Westbridge for about 2 weeks.
