## DM Diggin

### What You Know
- Tortles are natural humanoids that mostly live on the island of Chult (a large tropical island off the sothwestern coast of Faerun). They often worship the Sun and Moon because of its effect on their life-cycle (this is legit cannon lore, not something I added in to suit my campaign).

### Questions
- You were a monk at the secluded Abby of the Golden Sheath in the Sunset Mountains. You were surrounded by devouts who worshiped Chauntea, the Goddess of Agriculture. What was your role as a monk in this abby? Are you a worshiper of Chauntea? Were you at the abby for other reasons? What role do monks play at the Abby of the Golden Sheath? Protectors? Spiritual guides?

- How did you end up in the Sunset Mountains? Were you born there from an egg? Did your parents bring you here? Did you travel of your own accord?

### Special Features and Items
- **Curse of Yeenoghu** - You have a compulsion to eat reptilian animals (specifically reptilian animals, not dragonborn (huminoid) or dragons (dragon)). If a reptilian animal that you can see dies, you must succeed a DC 15 CON saving throw. On a failure, you hear abyssal whispers and you must attempt to consume the reptilian animal.

- **Tortle Instincts** When you gain the feared condition, the Shell Defense feature automatically activates and you retreat into your shell. At the beginning of your turn, you can make a DC 10 WIS saving throw to attempt to emmerge from your shell. On a success, you are still affected by the feared condition, but you are no longer in your shell.
