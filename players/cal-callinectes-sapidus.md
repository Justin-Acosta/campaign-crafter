# Callinectes Sapidus (Cal)

## 1. Stats
- **Race:** Human
- **Class:** Cleric (Twilight Domain)
- **Languages:** Common, Elvish, Goblin, Undercommon

## 2. Motivations
- **The Great Carcinization:** Cal fervently believes in a biological and magical inevitability: that all life is destined to evolve into crabs. He secretly (or not so secretly) works to validate this theory in all his research.
- **Magical Pathology:** He is deeply interested in afflictions, zoology, and magical phenomena that have religious or lunar connections (lycanthropy, vampirism, curses).
- **Curse dynamics:** He wants to understand how long-standing curses function. He isn't just there to break them; he wants to measure them, poke them, and see if they intensify or evolve over time.

## 3. Flaws
- **Crab-Colored Glasses:** He tends to interpret complex magical or emotional events through the lens of crustacean biology.
- **Scientific Detachment:** Cal views dangerous curses as specimens to be studied rather than just threats to be eradicated. He might "poke" a curse just to see what happens, prioritizing data over safety.
- **Emotional Proxy:** He relies on his ghostly crab pet to express strong emotions, often remaining outwardly even-tempered or detached himself.

## 4. Other Details
- **Holy Symbol & Pet:** His holy symbol is a crab pendant. A ghostly crab resides within it, often crawling on Cal or resting on his shoulder. This crab acts as an emotional outlet for Cal, reacting to the world when Cal remains stoic.
- **Chitinous Blessing:** The crab pendant allows Cal to cast *Barkskin* once a day (recharges at evening twilight). The spell manifests visually as plates of crab-like chitinous exoskeleton.
- **Temple Connections:** He is a "regular" at the [Kryptgarden Temple of Selune](../dungeons/kryptgarden-temple-of-selune.md) (or was, before the troubles). He knows High Priestess [Elara](../characters/kryptgarden-forest.md#elara) and her apprentice [Lyra](../characters/westbridge.md#lyra), having attended their astronomy lectures.
    - **Rivalry:** His crab has an adversarial relationship with an astronomer named Margrit Faulhorn and her rabbit familiar, Crescent (though Cal enjoys Margrit's tea).
- **Westbridge Contacts:** He has collaborated with the gnome artificer [Ghaliver Longstocking](../characters/westbridge.md#ghaliver-longstocking). Ghaliver has tinkered with Cal's instruments, and they share an intellectual rapport.
- **Background:** Born to parents who were researchers of niche magical esoterica. He has been in Westbridge for about 1-2 weeks investigating the current situation.
- **Twilight Affinity:** He has long been fascinated by the twilight, often gazing into the Underdark to appreciate that it might be gazing back.
