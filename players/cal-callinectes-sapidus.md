# Callinectes Sapidus (Cal)

## Initial Info

Human

- even tempered and this crab emotes for Cal with crabby gestures.
- cleric of the twilight domain. 
- I haven't decided on a race yet. 
- Holy Symbol: 
    - The gods associated with twilight are Selune, Illmater and, Helm.  
    - A crab pendant with the bound hands of Illmater, the moon representing Selune, and the armor of Helm.
    - Ghostly crab "pet" that resides in the pendant when it isn't crawling on Cal or resting on his shoulder to react to things or be an outlet for emotion. 

additional info:
- Cal is like a holy man of science. He is really interested in afflictions/zoology/magical phenomena that have an element of religion and/or the moon connected to them. 
- Secretly, he believes in carcinization where non-crab creatures evolve into crabs. He believes that everything someday could be crabs, and he will secretly work towards validating this theory. 

- I imagine he has been to the temple of Selune. 
- Probably knows some folks there and around Westbridge.
