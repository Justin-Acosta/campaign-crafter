## Player Respose

God/Goddess: , God of Agriculture
His faith to Chauntea is directly rooted in his upbringing at the Abbey. While he worships Chauntea and his thankful to her, he is not devout and hasnt been since he discovered Artificing. He tries to do right by her and the people who raised him and wouldnt want to do something he would think the goddess or worse, Father Gharant would disprove of. He still prays from the same prayer book given to him at the Abbey, and carries around her holy symbol. While they arent much, he considers these items of extreme importance to him.

Deep Gnome:
His identity as a Deep Gnome is mostly superficial, as he was raised completely at the Abbey.
While he always knew he was different, the people of the Abbey treated him no differently than the others (except when Father Gharant would ask him to help find something in the dark basement of the Abbey). Meeting Ghaliver was a big moment for him, having never seen something who looked even remotely like him, even though they were different subraces. He still harbors a fascination with other gnomes and knowing what they are like, but holds no deep rooted connection to his people or his origin.

Temple of Worship:
Nestled in a valley deep in the Sunset Mountains is the Abbey of the Golden Sheaf. This Abbey and surrounding small village sit in a valley with the right conditions for year long harvests. Discovered and blessed long ago by Chauntea herself (or so the story goes), the valley became a place of worship as generations of her kin and worshippers built the Abbey and tended to the fields. Now, the Clerics of the Golden Sheaf worship and train to protect the valley from intruders while the monks and clergy, alongside the small amount of villagers, till and harvest the field all year in the name of Chauntea, seeing it as their duty and stewardship. The grain and vegetables harvested here are sent and sold all over the sword coast. The Clerics also founded an orphanage, where they would take in wayward and lost children until they are of age, at which point they are given the chance to stay at the Abbey and join the Clerics in their duty or go out into the wide world. Father Gharant, was the head Cleric at the Abbey. A kind, gentle man whose devout faith in Chauntea emanated from every aspect of life at the Abbey. The Abbey had a reputation for being home to very welcoming and warm people, while also known as an impenetrable fortress because of its location in the mountains and the powerful Clerics who defended it.
Full backstory for more context:

Nestled in a valley deep in the Sunset Mountains is the Abbey of the Golden Sheaf. This Abbey and surrounding small village sit in a valley with the right conditions for year long harvests. The Clerics of the Golden Sheaf worship and train to protect the valley from intruders while the monks and clergy, alongside the small number of villagers, till and harvest the field all year in the name of Chauntea, seeing it as their duty and stewardship. The grain and vegetables harvested here are sent and sold all over the sword coast. The Clerics also founded an orphanage, where they would take in wayward and lost children until they are of age, at which point they are given the chance to stay at the Abbey and join the Clerics in their duty or go out into the wide world. 

Jareth, as a young deep gnome pup, was found by Father Gharant on the long road through the mountains to the Abbey. (Father Gharant knew that Jareth was probably left behind during a deep gnome migration, which he knew had recently happened). Knowing that the parents would be long gone or dead, he took the baby gnome in to raise in the Abbeys orphanage. 

Jareth grew slowly, as most gnomes do. At 10, Father Gharant saw potential in the boy, with his deep fascination for all things the Clerics did, and started training him to be a Cleric. At 20, after 10 years of training with the Clerics, Jareth met the first gnome to ever cross his path. An Artificer by the name of Ghaliver Longstocking stopped at the Abbey after getting lost, and the clerics graciously hosted him. Jareth immediately took a fascination in the gnome, who always had some gadget on him to show. Ghaliver showed Jareth how he used magic to give great power to his gadgets, as well as showing him how to take apart and put back together the pocket watch he carried with him. 

While Jareth had faithfully followed the ways of Chauntea for 10 years, something new brewed in him. Ghaliver told Jareth that when he is of age and leaves the Abbey to seek him out in Westbridge if he ever wishes to pursue Artificing further. After he left, Jareth went to work scouring the Abbey library for anything on artificing, finding several books on the subject. By 25, he had taken apart and mostly put back together every clock in the Abbey, much to the ire of the priests and clerics of the Abbey. By 30, he had become known as the guy in the valley who could fix anything, as long as you were okay with getting it back in some strange configuration. At 35, he found an alchemist set, and having read The Alchemical Focus of Artificing by Grimwald Dinklebottom, decided to start experimenting with chemicals. Father Gharant, who at this point had been nothing but supportive of Jareth's endeavors, reluctantly gave him a small room in the basement of the Abbey to be his repair room and now alchemical lab. It was close to his 38th birthday, when a chemical concoction he started ended in the Abbey basement in flames and structural damage to the Abbey, something that Jareth could not fix. Father Gharant, now an old man, resigned that this Abbey is too small for the ambitions of this gnome, made the choice for Jareth on his 38th birthday. Go out in the world and pursue artificing fully. 

Jareth, forever thankful to Father Gharant and Chauntea for the life he was given, left the Abbey with the promise to return one day when he his a master artificer. He sets off from the Sunset Mountain with Westbridge as his destination.
