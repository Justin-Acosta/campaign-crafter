# Jareth Brightbolt

## 1. Stats
- **Race:** Deep Gnome (Svirfneblin)
- **Class:** Artificer (Alchemist)
- **Deity:** Chauntea (God of Agriculture)
- **Background:** Acolyte / Guild Artisan hybrid (Raised in an Abbey, became a Tinkerer)
- **Languages:** Common, Gnomish, Undercommon

## 2. Motivations
- **Mastery of Artifice:** Jareth left his home to fully pursue the path of the Artificer. He aims to master the craft and eventually return to the Abbey to show Father Gharant what he has become.
- **The Mentor:** He specifically came to Westbridge to find [Ghaliver Longstocking](../characters/westbridge.md#ghaliver-longstocking), the gnome Artificer who first sparked his interest in technology and magic years ago.
- **Filial Piety:** Despite his shift away from divine magic, he strives to do right by the teachings of Chauntea and Father Gharant. He carries his prayer book and holy symbol and tries to live in a way that wouldn't shame his adoptive father.

## 3. Flaws
- **Reckless Tinkerer:** Jareth has a knack for deconstruction rather than reconstruction. His attempts to cast *Mending* result in the unique cantrip **Breaking**, which creates tears or breaks in objects rather than fixing them.
- **Volatile Methodology:** His experiments tend to be... energetic. He was essentially evicted from his home after an alchemical concoction set the Abbey basement on fire.
- **Superficial Heritage:** Raised entirely by humans in a surface abbey, he feels disconnected from his Deep Gnome roots. He is fascinated by other gnomes but lacks a deep cultural connection to his own people.

## 4. Other Details
- **Origin:** Raised in the **Abbey of the Golden Sheaf** in the Sunset Mountains. He was found as a baby by **Father Gharant**, the head cleric, and raised in the orphanage.
- **Magic & Mechanics:**
    - **Alchemy Components:** He MUST use alchemy components to cast any magic from his Alchemist spell list.
    - **Breaking Cantrip:** Can snap keys, tear cloaks, or break chain links (max 1 foot break). Can physically damage magic items but not remove their magic.
- **Connections:**
    - **Ghaliver Longstocking:** His role model and the reason he is in Westbridge.
    - **Fendrel:** Ghaliver has mentioned a hermit alchemist named [Fendrel](../characters/kryptgarden-forest.md#fendrel) living in Kryptgarden Forest. Fendrel is rumored to be an exile from Longsaddle with an explosive temper to match his potions—a potential kindred spirit or warning for Jareth.
- **Timeline:** Has been in Westbridge for about 2 weeks.
