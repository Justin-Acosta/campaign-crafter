# Jade

## 1. Stats
- **Race:** Green Dragonborn
- **Class:** Psionic Fighter
- **Background:** Criminal (Former Enforcer)
- **Languages:** Common, Draconic

## 2. Motivations
- **Ancestral Truth:** Jade is searching for the ancient green dragon [Old Gnawbone (Claugiyliamatar)](../characters/kryptgarden-forest.md#claugiyliamatar) in the Kryptgarden Forest. She hopes to learn about her unknown mother and her true last name, as green dragonborn are rare.
- **Fugitive's Sanctuary:** She is fleeing the fallout of a Lord's Alliance raid on her father's gang in Waterdeep. She aims to stay one step ahead of both the law and the Zhentarim.
- **Strategic Protection:** She is actively trying to ingratiate herself with the town guards at the Westbridge barracks (likely under [Captain Darian Hale](../characters/westbridge.md#captain-darian-hale)) to secure a layer of protection against any enforcers sent to retrieve or silence her.

## 3. Flaws
- **Looking Over Her Shoulder:** Jade is paranoid that her past is catching up to her. She likely views strangers with suspicion and checks exits when entering a room.
- **Enforcer's Instinct:** Having spent her life as muscle for a gang, she may default to intimidation or violence to solve problems that could be handled diplomatically.
- **Transactional Trust:** Her "friendship" with the guards is born of necessity, not genuine camaraderie, which might make her seem distant or manipulative.

## 4. Other Details
- **Family:**
    - **Father:** Raggmarrow (Half-orc), a gang leader in Waterdeep affiliated with the Zhentarim.
    - **Mother:** An unknown Green Dragonborn.
- **The Lead:** She was asking around for Old Gnawbone, and [Avon](../characters/westbridge.md#avon) suggested that the missing priestess [Elara](../characters/kryptgarden-forest.md#elara) might have information.
- **Timeline:** She has been in Westbridge for about 3 weeks.
- **Origin:** Waterdeep.
