# Westbirdge Side Quests

### The Worried Hunter

> Kaeleen, the stern-faced master archer who oversaw the Sunshot Archery Contest, approaches you. Her usual stoic demeanor is gone, replaced by a barely concealed anxiety.
>
> "You're the ones Avon trusted, yes? My hunting partner, Borin—the big man from the Sunstone Challenge—he went into the Kryptgarden Forest two days ago. He was convinced he could track those undead back to their source after his last party was attacked. He's a fool, but he's my fool. Please, I need your help to find him."

**Technical Information:**

- **Quest Hook:** Kaeleen can provide the party with a rough map of the area Borin was last seen in, marking a clear starting point for a search in the Kryptgarden Forest.
- **Reward:** If the party brings Borin back to Westbridge safely, a grateful Kaeleen will offer to train one party member. That party member gains 1 level of Ranger.
