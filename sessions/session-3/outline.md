> The Dawnray Spear:
This is a +1 spear that shines with the light of the morning sun when it strikes. If this spear deals damage to a creature or object, it shines with the light of dawn in a 30ft radius, illuminating the area and deals 1d6 radiant damage to all creatures within 5ft of the creature or object it hit. When it deals damage to an undead creature, it deals an additional 1d8 radiant damage.
