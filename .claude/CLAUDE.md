# Project Overview

A D&D campaign set in and around **Westbridge**, featuring a dual threat: undead from a corrupted **Temple of Selûne** in the **Kryptgarden Forest**, and the **Cult of Hadar** (the Heralds) — warlocks who abduct the town's beloved cleric **Avon** during the annual Eclipse Festival. The party of 5 PCs must investigate the temple corruption, contend with the cult, and navigate alliances across the region.

## Directory Structure

- `brainstorming/` — Campaign ideation, session-0 prep, and ongoing plot development notes
- `chapters/` — High-level campaign arc outlines (Chapter 1: Eclipse Festival, Chapter 2: Temple of Selûne)
- `characters/` — NPC rosters organized by location/faction (Westbridge, Cult of Hadar, Kryptgarden Forest, Sumber Hills)
- `creatures/` — Stat blocks for undead bestiary and custom NPCs (Heralds, allies, guards)
- `documents/` — In-world artifacts like letters and handouts
- `dungeons/` — Dungeon maps and room-by-room frameworks
- `loot-tables/` — d100 loot tables by region
- `lore/` — World-building and history for each region and faction
- `players/` — PC backstories and character details (Jade, Cal, Jareth, Tethys, Yomyd)
- `random-encounter-tables/` — Detailed encounter tables by region (Kryptgarden, Westbridge, Sumber Hills)
- `sessions/` — Per-session outlines, encounter details, and post-session summaries
- `side-quests/` — Optional quest hooks by region
- `preferences.md` — Writing style guidelines and content formatting rules
- `z-services/` — Python tooling for pulling stat blocks from Open5e
