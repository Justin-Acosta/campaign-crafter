# Session 0 Materials

This is a small collection of information about the Cult of Hadar campaign. This will provide some NPC's and going-ons organized by location that can provide inspiration for your character. ## Westbridge

Westbridge is a modest farming village that owes its name to its simple geography: it sits on the west side of the ancient Stone Bridge. Positioned at the crossroads of the Long Road and the Stone Trail, it serves as a vital hub for local commerce. The village features a central market, a bustling stockyard, and several warehouses. Though primarily an agricultural community, many residents are skilled hunters, often venturing into the nearby Kryptgarden Forest for game. For generations, the village has hosted the Eclipse Festival, a cherished tradition honoring both Lathander and Selûne, drawing visitors from miles around.

### Key Figures

*   **Avon:** A kind and respected human cleric of Lathander in his late fifties. He is the heart of the community in many ways, but has seemed more worried than usual lately, his eyes often drifting towards the Kryptgarden Forest. *(How might your character know Avon? Do you owe him a favor? Are you a fellow follower of Lathander?)*
*   **Ghaliver Longstocking:** A gnome artificer whose workshop, "Ghal's Gadgets," is a chaotic wonderland of whirring gears and half-finished inventions. Ghaliver is the town's go-to problem solver for anything mechanical, but their creations have a tendency to go haywire. *(Could your character be a former apprentice, a rival inventor, or someone who recently bought a gadget that spectacularly failed?)*
*   **Lyra:** A young silver dragonborn priestess of Selune, a refugee from the recently overrun temple in the forest. She is earnest and kind, but weighed down by worry for her missing mentor, Elara. *(Do you share a faith in Selûne? Perhaps you are from the Kryptgarden Forest and knew the temple before it fell?)*
*   **Kaelen and Borin:** A pair of human hunters who are well-known in Westbridge for their skill and bravery. Kaelen is a stern, master archer, while Borin is a burly, boisterous brawler. They often venture into Kryptgarden and know its dangers well. *(Are you a fellow hunter? Did you have a run-in with them in the forest? Perhaps you have a friendly rivalry?)*

### Going-ons

*   The annual Eclipse Festival is just around the corner, and the town is buzzing with a mixture of excitement and underlying dread.
*   Rumors are spreading about the Temple of Selune in the Kryptgarden Forest being overrun by undead. Refugees from the temple have started trickling into Westbridge, telling harrowing tales.
*   A hunting party went missing in the forest two tendays ago. While not an entirely uncommon occurrence, it has put the town on edge.

## Kryptgarden Forrest

An ancient, sprawling woodland known for its dense, tangled woods and a reputation for being notoriously dangerous. The forest's shadowy depths hide forgotten ruins and the lairs of many threats, drawing hunters and adventurers alike to its depths.

### Key Figures

*   **Claugiyliamatar ("Old Gnawbone"):** An ancient and cruel green dragon who lairs within the forest. She is more of a lurking threat and a dark legend than a common sight, but her influence is felt. She is cunning and prefers manipulation over direct confrontation. *(What stories have you heard of Old Gnawbone? Does the thought of her treasure-filled lair entice you, or does her terrifying reputation make you wary of the woods?)*
*   **Fell:** A mysterious firbolg merchant who seems to be a part of the forest itself. They sell items seemingly grown from the woods and speak in cryptic, leafy tones. *(Have you ever bartered with Fell? What strange, nature-infused item might you have acquired?)*
*   **Gideon Hawthorn:** A vain and arrogant human knight who can sometimes be found hunting the most exotic and beautiful creatures in the forest for trophies. He is skilled, but his pride often gets in his way. *(Have you ever crossed paths with Gideon? Did he mistake you for a commoner? Did you witness his flamboyant hunting style?)*

### Going-ons

*   Sightings of undead creatures have become increasingly common, particularly near the road to the Temple of Selune.
*   Strange, parasitic fungi have been seen growing in parts of the forest, creating eerie, glowing groves.
*   The fey creatures of the forest have become more reclusive and hostile, their usual trickery taking on a sharper, more dangerous edge.

## The Temple of Selune

A once-beautiful temple dedicated to the Moonmaiden, now a place of shadow and corruption. Built long ago by wood elves, it was a sanctuary of light and hope deep within the dangerous Kryptgarden Forest. It features open-air architecture to embrace the moonlight, reflecting pools for scrying and meditation, and extensive libraries.

### Key Figures

*   **Elara:** A respected wood elf priestess of Selune and the leader of the temple. She stayed behind when the temple was overrun and is now missing. She is a childhood friend of Avon from Westbridge. *(Did you ever visit the temple when it was safe? Did you know Elara or any of the other priests who served there?)*

### Going-ons

*   The temple has been overrun by undead, forcing the clergy to flee to Westbridge.
*   A powerful necrotic energy emanates from beneath the temple, corrupting the surrounding area.
*   The protective wards of Selune that once guarded the temple have fallen or been twisted into something malevolent.
