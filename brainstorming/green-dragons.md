# Green Dragon Information

- **Personality:** Green dragons are the most cunning and deceitful of true dragons, using misdirection and manipulation to get what they want. They are aggressive, loved to induce terror, and will often stalk their prey to learn their secrets before attacking.

- **Physical Description:** Green dragons have a jawline and crest that resembles a series of spikes, their eyes are small and piercing, and their scales are a vibrant green that darkens as they age. They have a distinctive chlorine-like smell that often precedes their arrival.

- **Preferred Terrain:** These dragons prefer ancient forests with towering trees, where the dim light and dense foliage provide ample cover. They often lair in caves behind waterfalls or in cliffside ruins.

- **Diet:** Green dragons are omnivorous, but they have a special fondness for elves and fey creatures. They will also eat other monsters, but they prefer to hunt intelligent prey.

- **Combat Tactics:** Green dragons are masters of ambush, using their breath weapon (a cone of poisonous gas) to weaken their enemies before engaging in melee. They will often use their magic to charm or dominate their foes, turning them against each other.

- **Lair Actions:** A green dragon fighting in its lair can call upon the forest to aid it. Grasping roots and vines can restrain enemies, and walls of thorns can block escape routes.

- **Regional Effects:** The forest within 1 mile of a green dragon's lair becomes warped by the dragon's magic. Trees grow twisted and thick, animals become aggressive spies for the dragon, and the air is often filled with a light, obscuring fog.

- **Treasure:** Green dragons love to collect art objects, especially those made of wood or emeralds. They also prize musical instruments and will often keep famous bards or artisans as "pets" to entertain them.

- **Claugiyliamatar ("Old Gnawbone"):** This ancient green dragon rules the Kryptgarden Forest and is known for her obsession with spying on the powerful women of Waterdeep. She yearns to walk among mortals in human form and uses crystal balls to watch events unfold in the city.

- **Minions:** Green dragons often employ kobolds, goblinoids, and other forest-dwelling creatures as minions. They also have a knack for charming animals and monsters to serve as guardians.

- **Parenting:** Green dragon parents are surprisingly attentive, teaching their wyrmlings the arts of deception and manipulation from a young age. However, this "care" is often a way to mold the young dragons into useful tools for the parents' own schemes.

- **Lore: The Chains of Abeir & The Arrival:**
    - **Abeir, the Twin World:** Toril has a twin world called Abeir. In Abeir, there are no gods to protect mortals. Instead, the world is ruled by the **Dragon Tyrants**—emperor-dragons who rule entire continents with absolute authority.

    - **Gauwervyndhal ("The Green Empress"):** The Green Dragon Tyrant of Abeir was Gauwervyndhal, ruler of the continent of Skelkor. She was not just a monster, but a paranoid, magic-obsessed empress who constantly shapeshifted to spy on her own court. She bred her dragonborn subjects to be the ultimate secret police, instilling in them a culture of surveillance and betrayal.

    - **Servants by Design:** In Abeir, dragonborn did not evolve naturally; they were bred by the Dragon Tyrants to be the perfect slave soldiers—loyal, strong, and elemental. Green dragonborn were designed by Green Dragon Tyrants to be spies, assassins, and secret police.

    - **The Spellplague:** About a century ago, a magical catastrophe called the Spellplague briefly merged the two worlds. During this chaos, the rebel dragonborn nation of **Tymanther** was ripped from Abeir and deposited onto Toril (Faerûn).
    - **Location in the Old Empires:** Tymanther is located in the "Old Empires" region of eastern Faerûn, displacing part of the ancient human kingdom of Unther. It lies roughly 2,000 miles southeast of the Sword Coast (where Westbridge and Kryptgarden are located). A Dragonborn adventurer in the Sword Coast is essentially on the other side of the continent, far from the support of their clan and the militaristic society of their homeland.
    
    - **The Cultural Trauma:** Because of this history, many dragonborn cultures have a deep-seated hatred for dragons. They do not worship them; they remember them as oppressors. A Green Dragonborn encountering Claugiyliamatar might not feel kinship, but rather the generational trauma of an escaped slave facing a master.
