## Here's what you can expect at session 0

### Vibe Check

1. I'll briefly talk about what you can expect out of the campaign
2. I'll talk about general themes you might encounter and how I like to run games
3. I'll ask all of you how you like to play TTRPG's

### Boundaries

1. We'll talk about any boundaries that people brought up in the player survey
2. I'll give all of you an opportunity to bring up boundaries / types of gameplay that make you uncomfortable

### Character Creation and Connections

1. You'll have an opportunity to create / finish your character sheet if needed
2. We'll spend the majority of this time talking about how your characters are connected to each other, the area in the campaign, and the NPC's in the campaign
3. This is also a time to bring up ideas you have. I'd like to incorporate your ideas into the campaign!

### Possible Short Combat Encounter

1. If time allows, we'll run a short combat where you can get a feel for your character's combat mechanics
2. This might inform any changes you decide to make before we start playing at Session 1
3. You can help me try out any character-specific abilities or items I give you
