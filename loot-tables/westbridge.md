## Technical Information

- **Harvesting:** Characters wishing to harvest parts from monstrosities or aberrations (like the Banderhobb or Star Spawn) must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Nature)** check. Success yields a **Monstrous Component** worth 1d10 x 10 gp (min 30 gp) to an alchemist or artificer.
- **Selling:** Most merchants in Westbridge are currently panicked or closed. Selling non-essential items might be difficult until the town stabilizes. [Captain Hale](../characters/westbridge.md#captain-darian-hale) or [Ghaliver Longstocking](../characters/westbridge.md#ghaliver-longstocking) may purchase useful supplies at full price.
