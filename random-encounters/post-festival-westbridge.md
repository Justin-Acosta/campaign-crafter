# Westbridge Random Encounters

This table can be used for random encounters in the town of Westbridge in the days following the Eclipse Festival and the abduction of Avon.

---

### d12 Encounters

---

### 1. One Last Game

> As you pass through the emptying market square, you spot one of the festival stalls still standing, though mostly packed. The proprietor, a cheerful gnome tinkerer with soot-smudged cheeks (Pippin), is carefully placing enchanted candles into a large crate. He spots you and his face lights up.
>
> "One last game for the heroes of the festival?" he asks, his voice a rapid-fire burst of excitement. He gestures to a familiar sight: the ghostly, shimmering antlers of a celestial elk. "The Moon-Elk Antler Toss! Special prize for a special round, just for you. Same rules as before, three rings, two successful throws to win. But this time, you'll get one of my own creations!"

**Technical Information:**

- **Challenge:** A character must succeed on two of three DC 15 ranged attack rolls to win.
- **Prize:** Pippin awards the winner a **Glimmering Lantern**. This bullseye lantern, when lit, functions normally but also reveals spectral outlines of any creature that has passed through its light in the last minute.
- **Guiding Question:** Does the party's interaction with Pippin draw any unwanted attention from the other nervous townsfolk?

### 3. Ghaliver's Gadget Gone Haywire

> A series of frantic metallic chirps and a woman's surprised shriek draw your attention to a nearby alley. You see one of Ghaliver Longstocking's clockwork critters—a tiny, steam-powered bird—flying in erratic circles, snatching bits of laundry from a clothesline and weaving them into a messy, flapping nest on a nearby roof.
>
> The bird's owner, a young woman, is desperately trying to coax it down. "Pip, you little menace! Bring back my smallclothes! Oh, thank the gods, can you help? I'm a weaver, not an adventurer. I can offer you a fine traveler's cloak if you can get my laundry back!"

**Technical Information:**

- **Challenge:** The malfunctioning clockwork bird can be brought down with a DC 14 Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) check to catch it, a DC 12 Intelligence (Arcana) check to disrupt its enchantment, or by dealing enough damage to knock it out of the air.
- **Reward:** The owner, Lina, gives the party a **traveler's cloak** (worth 10 gp). If the broken critter is returned to Ghaliver Longstocking, he offers a discount on a future purchase.
- **Guiding Question:** How does the party choose to deal with the malfunctioning creature? Do they use brute force or a more delicate touch?

### 4. The Whispering Well

> You find yourselves drawn to the Eclipse Well, the site of the dramatic abduction. The intricate metal framework of rings and symbols stands stark against the sky. As you draw closer, you hear it—faint whispers seeming to emanate from the stone well itself. The voices are faint and distorted, speaking in a language you don't recognize. The well was a focal point for powerful magic, and it now holds a strange resonance from the warlocks' telepathic communication. Around the base of the well, you spot a small, dark grey stone, cool to the touch, that seems to hum with the same energy.

**Technical Information:**

- **Challenge:** A DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana) check reveals the whispers are a telepathic echo. A successful check allows a character to make out a single, repeated word: "Hadar."
- **Item:** The stone is an **Echo Stone**. If held, it faintly hums when within 100 feet of a creature using telepathic communication. This requires a DC 12 Wisdom (Perception) check to find if the players don't approach the well.

### 6. The Calamity Collector (Merchant)

> Tucked away in a quiet corner of the market, a new stall has appeared. It is draped in black cloth and run by a single, enigmatic figure (Xylos). He is a tall, gaunt human with pale skin and eyes the color of a stormy sky. He wears immaculate black robes and speaks in a low, measured whisper. His wares are strange and unsettling, items scavenged from places touched by great magical events or disasters.
>
> "I'm a collector of... post-calamity curiosities."
>
> "Every tragedy leaves echoes," he says, his voice barely audible. "I simply find them. Perhaps you'll find something to your liking." He gestures to his wares.

**Technical Information:**

**Wares:**
**Last Hearthstone (200 gp):** Xylos gestures to a fist-sized, smooth river stone that is unnaturally warm and bears a single, faintly glowing rune.

> "The last warmth of a village's enchanged hearth, defiant against a dragon's frost. A small comfort against the cruel cold."

- Once per day, the holder can speak a command word to create a 15-foot radius of warmth, protecting all within from the effects of extreme cold for 8 hours.
  **Wraith-Light Lantern (500 gp):** Xylos carefully lifts a battered, salt-stained lantern made of dark, heavy iron. The glass is cloudy and seems to swirl with an inner fog.
  > "This light shone on a ship of fools who sought to chart a sea of ghosts. It still remembers how to see what is not there."
- Once per day, the user can light this lantern with a command word. For 10 minutes, the lantern casts a dim, grey light in a 30-foot radius. Within this light, the user can see creatures obscured by magic like that granted by the Invisibility spell.
  **Pyre-Knight's Gauntlet (1000 gp):** He indicates a single, scorched plate-steel gauntlet, blackened as if it was pulled from a fire. The leather beneath the steel is cracked but intact.
  > "This belonged to a knight who chose to burn with his king. It will not feel the flame again, but it has not forgotten the pain."
- The wearer of this gauntlet can cast the `Absorb Elements` spell as a reaction (proficiency bonus) times per long rest. When they do, the gauntlet glows with faint embers, and the wearer feels a phantom sensation of intense heat, causing them to have disadvantage on concentration saving throws.

### 7. A Warlock's Remnant

> As you walk down a street near the Eclipse Well, a glint of purple catches your eye. Tucked into the mud and grime of the road is a small, intricately embroidered piece of deep indigo cloth, clearly torn from a larger garment. It is stained with a few drops of the now-familiar black ichor. It seems to have been torn from the robes of one of the warlocks during the struggle of the abduction.

**Technical Information:**

- **Challenge:** A DC 12 Intelligence (Investigation) check reveals a faint, otherworldly scent clinging to the fabric—a mix of brimstone and something acrid, like ozone.
- **Item:** The cloth itself is a clue. It could be used to help a ranger track the warlocks (advantage on the next survival check to track them), or shown to Lyra or another knowledgeable figure who might recognize the specific embroidery style.
- **Guiding Question:** What does the party do with this clue? Do they try to investigate it themselves or bring it to the attention of the town's remaining leadership?

### 8. Spooked Beasts

> A sudden commotion erupts from the town's stockyard. Two large draft horses, their eyes wide with panic, have broken free from their enclosure and are now galloping through the streets. A gnome farmer (Finnan), his face a mask of frustration, is chasing after them. "Whoa there, Daisy! Stop, Petunia!" He looks over at you.
>
> "Don't just stand there! Help me wrangle these horses!"

**Technical Information:**

- **Challenge:** The horses can be calmed with a DC 13 Wisdom (Animal Handling) check or stopped with a DC 14 Strength (Athletics) check to grab their reins.
- **Reward:** The owner, Finnan, is incredibly grateful. He offers the party a bottle of his homemade cider. He explains that he makes a special batch every year after the harvest, using apples from a tree that was supposedly blessed by a traveling cleric of Lathander. A creature who drinks this cider gains 5 temporary hit points and has advantage on saving throws against being frightened for 1 hour. The bottle contains enough for four servings.
